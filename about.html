About Me

Pumiput Chaichat, Software Engineer

Hello, my name is Phumiphat, but you can call me Game. I graduated from Thammasat University, Rangsit Campus. I am currently working as a Software Engineer at an automotive parts manufacturing company in Eastern Thailand.

My responsibilities cover almost the entire software development process. I work directly with users and department managers to gather requirements, analyze problems, design databases and system architecture, develop both Frontend and Backend, deploy applications to the company server, and continue maintaining and improving them after they go live.

Whenever I develop a system, I always ask myself how it can reduce workload, save time, or reduce the company’s use of resources. At the same time, the system has to be easy for employees to use. Many of the projects I work on involve transforming processes that people have been familiar with for years. For example, replacing physical document approval with Electronic Approval, or transforming an annual employee evaluation process from paper-based forms and manual calculations into an online system where scores can be calculated and tracked in one place.

Working as a Software Engineer has taught me much more than just programming. I have learned how to communicate with users, understand the workflows of different departments, understand the company environment, and design software around the people who actually use it. I believe these skills are an important part of being a Software Engineer.

Free Time

Free Time

01

Reading

I enjoy reading in my free time, especially books about technology and personal development. I also like exploring articles and learning about things I haven't come across before. Reading gives me a chance to slow down, clear my mind, and sometimes even find new ideas that I can bring into my work.

02

Running

Running is one of my favorite ways to unwind after work. I usually go for an evening run to clear my head after spending most of the day in front of a computer. Some days I run longer, while other days I keep it short. I'm not always focused on speed or breaking records. I just enjoy staying consistent and seeing myself improve over time.

03

Gym

I really enjoy going to the gym and lifting weights. Besides staying healthy, I love seeing myself get stronger and being able to do things I couldn't do before. Some days are harder than others, but looking back at how much I've improved always makes the effort worth it. It's something I genuinely enjoy and want to keep getting better at.

04

Hiking

Whenever I get the chance, I love going hiking, especially when there's a beautiful sunset waiting at the top. The climb can be tiring, and sometimes I need to stop and catch my breath, but reaching the viewpoint makes it all worth it. I really enjoy being surrounded by nature, getting some exercise, and taking photos of the scenery along the way.

05

Travel

I love traveling, and whenever I have some free time, I'm usually looking for somewhere new to visit. Whether it's a short trip somewhere in Thailand or traveling abroad, I enjoy planning trips, trying new food, and exploring different places. Sometimes I plan everything in advance, while other times I just go with the flow. For me, traveling is all about enjoying the experience and collecting memories along the way.

06

Cafés

I enjoy exploring different cafés whenever I have some free time. Sometimes I visit a place because of its atmosphere, sometimes for the coffee, and sometimes just because I want somewhere different to relax. I especially like quiet cafés where I can enjoy a cup of coffee, listen to music, or read a book without feeling rushed. It's one of my favorite ways to spend a day off.

07

Food

I'm a big fan of good food, especially when I get to try new restaurants with friends or people close to me. It doesn't matter whether it's a small street food spot or a restaurant I've been wanting to visit. Sometimes I'll even drive quite far just to try a particular place. For me, enjoying a good meal with great company is one of the best ways to relax after a busy week.

08

Gardening

I also enjoy gardening when I'm at home, especially growing fruit trees. I like taking care of them and watching them grow from small plants until they eventually start producing fruit. Some grow quickly, while others take much longer, but there's something really satisfying about finally harvesting fruit from a tree I've grown myself. It's a relaxing hobby that teaches me to be patient and enjoy the little things.

The Path

The Path

  1. 01

    Kindergarten

    Looking back at my kindergarten days, life was pretty simple. Going to school, playing with friends, and waiting for the school day to end were probably my biggest concerns back then. I didn't have much to worry about and could enjoy all the little things. Seeing these old photos now always brings a smile to my face.

  2. 02

    Primary School

    Primary school was when life started getting a little busier. There were more classes, school activities, and plenty of new friends to meet. I got to try different things and made so many memories with my classmates, from playing during lunch breaks to joining school events. Looking back, those were some really fun and carefree years of my childhood.

  3. 03

    Lower Secondary

    Lower secondary school was when I started growing up and discovering more about myself. Friends became a huge part of my everyday life, whether we were studying, joining school activities, or just hanging out together. I started exploring different interests and figuring out what I liked and didn't like. I wasn't thinking too seriously about the future yet, but I made a lot of great memories during those years.

  4. 04

    Upper Secondary

    Upper secondary school was when I started thinking more seriously about my future and what I wanted to do after graduation. But there were still plenty of fun moments, from sports day and school activities to military cadet training with my friends. Some days were exhausting, while others were full of laughter. Looking back at these photos reminds me of how many great memories I made during those years.

  5. 05

    University

    After finishing high school, I went to Thammasat University, Rangsit Campus. At first, I had to adjust to a lot of new things, from meeting new friends to getting used to university life. I got to try different activities, take on more responsibilities, and experience things I'd never done before. But my favorite part was definitely spending time with friends, traveling, joining activities, and making memories together. Looking back, university was one of the most memorable chapters of my life, and I'd love to experience those days all over again.

  6. 06

    Graduation

    Finally, graduation day arrived! After years of studying, coding, and working on countless projects, I graduated with a Bachelor's degree from Thammasat University. It was such a special day, especially getting to celebrate with my family and friends who had been there throughout the journey. Looking at these photos reminds me of all the hard work, late nights, and great memories that brought me to this moment.

  7. 07

    Work

    After graduating, I started my career as a Software Engineer. Going from writing code for university assignments to building systems that people actually use at work was a big change. I quickly learned that being a developer isn't just about writing code. It's also about understanding users, solving real problems, and finding ways to make things work better. Every project comes with different challenges. Some problems are easy to solve, while others take days to figure out. Even though the work can be challenging, I genuinely enjoy building new systems and seeing people use something I've created.
